Our goals this year

  • A healthy, positive community where parents, students, and staff know and support each other.
  • A better experience for students and teachers through funding for classrooms, shared spaces, and learning materials.
  • Events that bring us together — like the Gala and the Jog-a-Thon — that raise funds and unite the St. Peter school, church, and Lodi community.

How you can help

Helping the PTL doesn't have to mean stacking folding chairs, though we love that too. The most valuable thing you can bring is whatever you're already good at.

  • Skills and expertise — graphic design, photography and videography, social media, apparel and online sales, IT and technical help, and construction or trades expertise for campus projects.
  • Time and hands — volunteering at events, leading an event, chairing a committee, or helping source auction and raffle items.
  • Resources and connections — event gear to lend, trusted vendor relationships, and sponsorship connections through your employer or local Lodi businesses.

With a large group, small contributions of time and talent add up — and a better-funded, more vibrant school is the one your children walk into every morning.

Meetings

We meet for one hour on the second Tuesday of every month at 6:00 PM in the School Library — the same day as Two Can Tuesday. Join in person, or on Google Meet if getting to campus is hard that night.

At each meeting, school administration gives a campus update, we review the PTL budget, vote as a group on staff funding requests, coordinate upcoming events, and leave open time for new ideas. This year we're starting each meeting with quick introductions, so it's one of the easiest ways to meet other parents, guardians, and teachers.

Get connected

The single best next step is to come to a meeting. It's one hour, and you'll hear the admin update, meet other families, and find the way you want to get involved.

  • Volunteer calls and event needs go out through school email, and we send flyers home too.
  • Event sign-ups are managed through SignUpGenius.
  • Want to lead an event, chair a committee, or offer a skill? Tell any board member, or raise your hand at the meeting.

The PTL isn't someone else doing things for the school — it's us. Parents, guardians, teachers, and staff, working together to make St. Peter Lutheran School the best it can be for our kids.
